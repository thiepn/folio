# Folio — Final Visual Polish Contract

Identity comes from the near-black neutral canvas, editorial serif hierarchy, precise mono metadata, thin rules, low-radius geometry, restrained information density, contextual disclosure, and quiet surface depth. Accent is state and decoration rather than the design language.

Canonical geometry: 2px micro, 4px controls, 6px panels. Shadows are primarily for overlays. Task/review/history collections use rows rather than card grids. Empty states stay quiet. Forecast and Review read as editorial reports rather than SaaS KPI dashboards. Motion is functional and brief; Phase 20 reduced-motion behavior remains authoritative.

Phone layout stays a distinct composition: fixed bottom navigation, edge-to-edge panels where useful, Day-first Calendar, swipe/snap Week cards, sheet overlays, >=44px touch targets, and no type shrinking merely to imitate desktop density.
